Frequently asked questions about learning
Chinese at Thousand Li online school

Isn’t it better to study with a native Chinese speaker?

A native speaker is great for conversation practice, but can’t explain the rules. Chinese is their mother tongue; they never learned it as a system. Ask “why this character here and not a similar one?” and the honest answer is “that’s just how we say it”. Hence the typical story: a year of lessons, decent pronunciation and a set of phrases, yet the person can’t express their own thoughts.

Our teacher has gone all the way from zero to diplomatic level. Grammar and the difference between similar words are explained in Russian — until you understand. And the Chinese you hear is native-level.

What if I miss a lesson?

Let your teacher know in advance — we’ll move the lesson to a time that suits you. Nothing will be lost: at the next lesson we’ll review what you need to continue.

How many lessons do I need?

It depends on your goal and starting level. At the free trial lesson we’ll assess your level and make a plan: how many lessons it will take to start speaking, pass the HSK level you need or conduct business negotiations.

I have studied Chinese before but can’t really speak. Is this for me?

Yes, it’s a common situation: you know words and characters, but can’t build a sentence. We’ll go through the system of the language in Russian, fill the gaps in grammar and add conversation practice with a Chinese teacher.

What do I need for lessons?

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and 60 minutes. Your teacher selects the learning materials.
